Color Sequence – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Color Sequence Game ("the game") is an Apple Watch game made by Halil Kaya ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses.

We collect no data

The game does not collect, send or share any personal or usage data. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting services and no in-app purchases. It does not connect to the internet.

Information stored on your watch

Your best score is saved only on your Apple Watch. We never receive it. Deleting the game deletes it.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
